## v1.7.1 - Your progress

### New
- **Level and XP:** every finished run earns XP from coins, golden coins, stages and near-misses. Your level (up to 50) is shown in the main menu with an XP bar.
- **Daily missions:** three new missions every day (easy, medium, hard), the same for everyone. Complete all three on consecutive days to build a streak.
- **Game history:** your last 20 games with date, mode, score, stage and time, plus averages. "Copy data" copies them as a table you can paste anywhere.
- **Progress screen** with level, missions, streak and history
- 10 new achievements (25 in total)

### Notes
- Your records, stats and skins are kept. If you already played, your starting XP is calculated from your total coins, golden coins and near-misses.
- Saves are updated to a new version, with an automatic backup first.
- Runs you quit from the pause menu do not give XP and do not count for missions.
