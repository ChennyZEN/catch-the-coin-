## v1.6.1 - Windows version

### New
- **Windows app (.exe):** a portable build, just download and double-click. No installation needed.
- Press **F** for fullscreen, just like in the browser

### Notes
- No gameplay changes in this update
- Progress (records, stats, skins) is saved separately in each version (browser, installed app and Windows app)
- The app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
