## v1.5.0 - Skins and Customize

### New
- **Character skins:** a new **Customize** screen lets you pick your look. Classic is always available, **Green** unlocks at 500 total coins and **Pink** at 1000.
- Total coins count every coin you catch across all your runs (see Stats)
- An in-game notice when you unlock a skin, and a recap at the end of the run
- New achievement: **Wardrobe** (unlock every skin), for 11 achievements in total
- Stats now show how many skins you have unlocked

### Changed
- The main menu artwork is a single spinning coin again

### Notes
- Your records, stats and achievements are kept
- If you use "Reset data", your skin goes back to Classic
