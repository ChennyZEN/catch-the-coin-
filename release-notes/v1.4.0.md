## v1.4.0 - Game modes, stats and achievements

### New
- **Four game modes:** Classic, **Daily Challenge** (the same run for everyone each day), **Time Attack** (60 seconds, every bat costs 5 seconds) and **Zen** (90 relaxed seconds, no bats, coin showers)
- **Statistics:** games, time played, coins, golden coins, power-ups, best combo, best stage and a record for each mode
- **10 achievements** with in-game notifications
- New **How to Play**, **Stats** and **Settings** screens (volumes, sound, language, reset data)
- **English and Italian** interface, picked from your browser and changeable in Settings
- Press **1-4** in the menu to choose a mode

### Notes
- The Daily Challenge uses your local date
- Your previous Classic record is kept
- Zen ramps difficulty up more slowly

Play online: https://chennyzen.itch.io/catch-the-coin
