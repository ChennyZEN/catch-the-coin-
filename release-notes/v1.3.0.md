## v1.3.0 - Power-ups, combos and waves

### New
- **Power-ups:** shield (absorbs one bat), magnet (pulls coins), slow-mo
- **Golden coin** worth 5 points, rare and glowing
- **Combo multiplier:** catch coins in a row to reach x5
- **New bats:** zig-zag and fast ones, with a warning mark before they enter
- **Stages every 25 coins:** themed backgrounds (cave, night, storm) and warning waves that always leave a free lane
- The game music speeds up a bit at each stage

### Notes
- Difficulty now scales with the coins you catch, not with your points
- Scoring changed, so records from older versions are not comparable
