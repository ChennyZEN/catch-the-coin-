## v1.7.0 - Risk and reward

### New
- **Near-miss:** slip past a bat without touching it for bonus points and a "CLOSE!" pop. Chain them for more. In Time Attack each one gives +0.5 seconds.
- **Fever:** coins, golden coins and near-misses fill the bar at the top. When it is full you get 7 seconds of double points, a stronger magnet and more golden coins... and more bats.
- **Biome rules:** Night limits your vision (everything glows so you can still see it), Storm brings wind gusts with a warning
- **Hand-designed waves** with names (Wall, Stairs, Arrowhead, Snake, Pincer, Checkerboard). Each one has a safe path, shown by a coin trail.
- **New difficulty curve:** a gentler start, rising pressure, and a breather after each wave. A new stage every 20 coins.
- **More impact:** a quick hit-stop and a slow-motion death where your character shatters
- 4 new achievements and new stats
- Settings: "Biome effects" switch (always on in Daily)

### Notes
- Your records, stats and skins are kept. Saves now have a version number and a backup is made before they are updated.
- In Daily, every item still comes from the same seed for everyone, but waves, wind and power-ups follow your own progress.
