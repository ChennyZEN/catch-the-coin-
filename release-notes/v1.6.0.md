## v1.6.0 - Install it, feel it

### New
- **Installable app (PWA):** open https://chennyzen.github.io/catch-the-coin-/ and install the game on your phone or computer. It works offline once loaded. There is also an **Install app** button in Settings when your browser supports it.
- **Smoother controls:** the character now accelerates and brakes softly, with the same feel at 60, 120 or 144 Hz
- **Adaptive quality:** if your device struggles, glow and particles are reduced automatically
- **More animation:** the player breathes, squashes when it catches something and kicks up dust; coins glint; the golden coin has orbiting sparkles; bats sway, zig-zag bats lean and fast bats leave a trail; power-ups float with a spinning ring; menus fade in
- Slow-mo now eases in and out and the score counts up smoothly

### Notes
- Progress (records, stats, skins) is saved separately on itch.io and on the GitHub Pages version
- If your system asks for reduced motion, screen shake is turned off

Play online: https://chennyzen.itch.io/catch-the-coin
